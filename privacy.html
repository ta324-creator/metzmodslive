Privacy Policy

What this site does and doesn't do with your information, in plain language.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

METZ MODS ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at metzmods.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit, how it's used, and your choices.

The short version

This Site does not have a contact form, does not require an account, and does not process payments online. We do not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal details through the Site. The only outside service embedded on the Site is Google Maps, and the Site links out to WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram if you choose to use them.

Information collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, standard technical information — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited — may be logged automatically by our hosting provider as part of normal website operation and security. This information is not used to identify you personally and is not sold or shared for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps. Some pages embed a Google Maps view to help you find our shop. Loading this embed may allow Google to set cookies or collect data in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control what Google does with this data.
  • WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram. The Site links to our WhatsApp number and social media profiles. Clicking these links takes you to that platform, where its own privacy policy and terms apply.
  • Hosting. The Site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Standard server logs may be retained by our hosting provider under their own privacy practices.

Cookies

The Site itself does not set first-party advertising or tracking cookies. The embedded Google Maps feature may set cookies from Google as described above.

Children's privacy

This Site is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because we don't collect personal information directly through the Site, there's generally no personal data on file with us to access, correct, or delete. If you've contacted us by phone, text, or WhatsApp, that's handled the same way as any other business communication — you're welcome to contact us directly if you'd like us to disregard or delete something you've shared with us that way.

California residents

California law, including the CCPA/CPRA, gives residents certain rights regarding personal information collected by businesses that meet specific size or revenue thresholds. METZ MODS does not sell personal information and does not use the Site to collect personal information as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
