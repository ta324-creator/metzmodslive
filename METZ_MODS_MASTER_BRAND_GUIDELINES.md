# METZ MODS — MASTER BRAND + CONTENT GUIDELINES

## PURPOSE
Give any future Claude/AI enough context to keep creating METZ MODS content with the same brand direction, vibe, and knowledge established in this conversation.

## BRAND
**Name:** METZ MODS  
**Industry:** Automotive repair, maintenance, trucks, performance, and modifications.

**Core positioning:**
**GENERAL AUTO MAINTENANCE + TRUCK / PERFORMANCE / MODS**

METZ MODS must NOT look like a performance-only shop.

It should appeal to:
- Everyday daily-driver owners
- Routine maintenance customers
- Brake/fluid/oil/basic service customers
- Truck owners
- Lift/level-kit customers
- Performance/modification customers

The brand can have a motorsport/performance attitude while still feeling approachable to normal vehicle owners.

## VIBE
Automotive • Motorsport • Racing • Fast • Mechanical • Clean • Bold • Modern • Industrial • Slightly aggressive • Professional • Decal/sticker-friendly.

Avoid:
- Corporate/generic auto-shop feel
- Cheap/cartoonish design
- Overly futuristic design
- Luxury/exotic-car aesthetic
- Complicated/cluttered layouts

The identity should feel like it could naturally exist as an automotive racing decal, shop badge, vehicle graphic, apparel mark, or social-media brand.

## LOGO DIRECTION
Preferred logo structure is a **single horizontal line**:

`[RACING STRIPES] METZ MODS`

No stacked/broken logo. No wavy text. No curved text.

### Racing stripes
Use forward-moving motorsport stripes inspired by the general idea of racing stripes, but create an original METZ MODS treatment. Do NOT copy Toyota's exact logo/trademarked design.

### METZ
Bold, strong, clean, angular/automotive. Slight forward slant is okay.

### MODS
Heavy uppercase lettering with horizontal speed-line/cut effects. Fast and dynamic but readable. Inspired by the *concept* of Speedhunters-style motion typography, NOT a copy of its logo.

## LOGO COLOR DIRECTION
A strong explored direction:
- Black rectangular box
- White logo
- White racing stripes
- High contrast
- Clean racing/decal appearance

Other applications can use black, white, metallic/silver, or restrained motorsport accents.
